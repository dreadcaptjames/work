What you get

Core recommendation

Direct connection, presentation switcher, matrix or AV over IP, chosen from your display count, routing and cable runs.

Signal-flow diagram

Sources, core, displays and audio chain, with HDMI / HDBaseT / fiber and ENC/DEC marked on each run.

Real equipment list

Encoders, decoders, switchers, DSPs, sized amplifiers, mics, cameras and control, each with a model picker.

Open-item flags

Unrouted displays, missing distances, conferencing without mics and other gaps get called out.

Export anything

BOM as CSV, diagram as SVG, the whole sketch as PDF, and the project as JSON to reopen later.

Private by default

Runs entirely in your browser. Your designs are stored locally and never leave your device.
